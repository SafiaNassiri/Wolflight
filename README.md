# WolfLight

**WolfLight** is a 2D atmospheric action-adventure game about decay, memory, and a world abandoned by its own moon. You play as a young child wandering through a land consumed by a creeping corruption — a physical manifestation of a world that has forgotten love, guidance, and gentle light. As the moon’s glow vanished, so did the warmth that once protected the land. Now only faint remnants of that light remain, carried within the player.

---

## Premise

Long ago, the moon watched over the world, nurturing it like a mother to her child. When she vanished, the land began to rot, forests withered, and monstrous silhouettes appeared in every shadow.  
You are a child born after the moon’s disappearance — having never seen her glow nor felt her love. The spreading decay reflects this absence: a world growing up without its guardian.

Your journey is a search for the fragments of moonlight scattered across the world, each one a memory of warmth long lost.

---

## Planned Core Features

- **Atmospheric Exploration**  
  Traverse haunting forests, ruined villages, and forgotten temples consumed by moonless decay.

- **Light-Based Mechanics**  
  Use shards of moonlight as tools, weapons, and ways to push back the corruption.

- **Story Through Environment**  
  Learn the world’s history by observing environmental changes, visual symbolism, and subtle narrative cues.

- **Shadow Creatures**  
  Enemies formed from the absence of love and guidance — lingering echoes of what once thrived.

- **Soft Narrative Themes**  
  Symbolism of childhood, abandonment, healing, and rebuilding what was lost.

---

## Lore Notes (In Progress)

- The decay is *not* evil — it’s grief made physical.  
- The child is connected to the moon but doesn’t know how or why.  
- The moon vanished suddenly, leaving the world to raise itself.  
- Some creatures remember the moon; others resent her disappearance.  
- Moonlight fragments show visions of the past: brightness, love, and things worth restoring.
